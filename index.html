The Free Game
That Wasn't

A three o'clock Florida storm, a bored family on the couch, and a bright new game on the big TV that costs nothing to play. Castle Kingdoms rains gold coins, flashes a countdown, and dares you not to miss a day.

Chase is all in. Ash starts asking the questions nobody else is asking. One by one, the Wilders figure out the oldest trick there is — dressed up in new colors.

The lesson

"Nothing is free. Somebody always pays. If you can't see who — look in the mirror."

Why this series exists

Kids learn plenty in school. They just never learn how the world actually works.
